## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- Initial release of TSnooze.
- Automatic inactivity-based tab suspension for Chrome/Edge Manifest V3.
- Popup controls for enable/disable, delay selection, and wake-all behavior.
- Per-document Google account pinning for Google Workspace documents.
- Whitelist support for hostnames and subdomains.
